Bubble Gum Fellow

"Generational Spearhead"

About Bubble Gum Fellow

Bubble Gum Fellow was active from 1995-1997. He was one of the four horse from the 1996 Classic generation, but faced complications for his Triple Crown. He won the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and the first 3 year old since 1936.

Agnes Tachyon

"The Phantom Triple Crown"

About Agnes Tachyon

Agnes Tachyon was active from 2000 to 2001. He was one of the five horses from the "Strongest Generation". He retired after the Satsuki Sho in 2001, with Jungle Pocket winning the Japanese Derby and Manhattan Cafe winning the Kikuka Sho.

Espoir City

"Pure-Hearted × Rebellious Girl"

About Espoir City

Espoir City was active from 2008 to 2013. Espoir City would have multiple races tiles like; the best Japanese dirt horses of the era, winning two JRA and seven NAR Grade 1 dirt races.

Special Week

"Ruler of Japan" AKA : "Supreme Commander of Japan"

About Special Week

Special Week was active from 1997 to 1999. Fans called him the "Supreme Commander of Japan". Late he became a prolific and broodmare sire, with his bloodline still in many Japanese horses today.
