Biography

Hi, I am @applicant10008_.
My name comes from a character called Agent 8 in Splatoon 2 and I am an Umamusume fan.



Why did I choose these games?

It's because they are my current favorites and I have loved them since I was a teenager. Why quotes on the games? The quotes represent famous lines from the games or the official lines/quotes of the games.



Why did I choose these Umamusume characters?

I choose these characters because they are my current favorites and some of the characters I hope are on the global version. The quotes that are under each of the characters is because they are their real life counterpart. For example for Agnes Tachyon her quote "The Phantom Triple Crown" is because she has won multiple G1 races without defeat there for "Triple Crown" but she suffered an injury at one of her races. Due to her injury she could not continue her career there for the name "Phantom" because people who saw her potential to continue on her career didn't get to see it.
